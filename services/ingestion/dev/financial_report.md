## 1. Executive Summary

Southern Cross Retail Group delivered a resilient result in FY2025 despite a
soft consumer environment in the second half. Group revenue rose 6.8% to
$4,213.4 million, driven by continued growth in the online channel and the
first full-year contribution from the Harbour Home acquisition. Underlying
EBIT increased 4.1% to $312.7 million, while statutory net profit after tax
declined 2.3% to $178.9 million, reflecting one-off integration costs and the
impairment of the Metro Express store network.

## 2. Operating and Financial Review

### 2.1 Segment Performance

The Group operates four reportable segments: Supermarkets, Home & Living,
Online Marketplace, and Financial Services.

| Segment | Revenue FY2025 | Revenue FY2024 | EBIT FY2025 | EBIT FY2024 | EBIT Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Supermarkets | $2,601.3m | $2,542.7m | $182.4m | $186.9m | 7.0% |
| Home & Living | $912.5m | $761.0m | $74.2m | $61.3m | 8.1% |
| Online Marketplace | $498.1m | $446.9m | $38.6m | $33.5m | 7.7% |
| Financial Services | $201.5m | $194.6m | $17.5m | $18.7m | 8.7% |
| **Group total** | **$4,213.4m** | **$3,945.2m** | **$312.7m** | **$300.4m** | **7.4%** |

**Supermarkets.** Comparable store sales grew 1.9%, below the 3.4% recorded in
FY2024, as customers traded down to private-label ranges. Private-label
penetration reached 31.2% of sales (FY2024: 28.6%). Fourteen stores were
refurbished during the year and three underperforming metropolitan sites were
closed.

**Home & Living.** The segment benefited from the first full-year contribution
of Harbour Home, acquired in March 2024. Excluding the acquisition, organic
revenue declined 2.1% in a subdued housing market. Integration synergies of
$11.3 million were realised against a full-run-rate target of $18 million by
FY2027.

**Online Marketplace.** Gross transaction value rose 15.7% to $1,092 million.
Active customers increased from 3.1 million to 3.6 million, and the
subscription program "SCR Plus" ended the year with 412,000 members
(FY2024: 268,000).

**Financial Services.** Receivables quality remained sound, with 90+ day
arrears of 1.14% (FY2024: 0.98%). EBIT declined due to higher funding costs,
partially offset by repricing of the co-branded credit card portfolio.

### 2.2 Group Outlook

The Group expects low single-digit revenue growth in FY2026, with cost
inflation moderating but consumers remaining value-conscious. Capital
expenditure guidance is $190–210 million, weighted toward supply chain
automation at the Kemps Creek distribution centre. The Metro Express network
review will conclude in the first half, with up to nine further closures under
consideration.

### Note 12 — Impairment

Following a strategic review, the carrying value of the Metro Express
cash-generating unit was impaired by $28.4 million ($19.9 million net of
tax), comprising $16.2 million of store fit-out assets, $9.1 million of
right-of-use assets, and $3.1 million of allocated goodwill. The recoverable
amount was determined on a value-in-use basis using a post-tax discount rate
of 9.8% and a terminal growth rate of 2.0%.
